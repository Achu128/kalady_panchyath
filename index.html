01 / About the Panchayat

Kalady Grama Panchayat

Kalady Grama Panchayat is located in the eastern part of Ernakulam district, Kerala, famously renowned as the birthplace of Sri Adi Shankara and situated on the sacred banks of the Periyar River. It is part of Aluva Taluk.

As a local self-government institution under LSGD Kerala, the Panchayat plays an essential role in local administration, civic infrastructure, sanitation, environmental conservation, water management, and community welfare.

Its geographical setting, riverine drainage system, road connectivity, settlement patterns, and natural resources provide critical baseline data for spatial analysis and decentralized panchayat-level planning.

03 / Geographic Setting

Location of Kalady

The location map provides a geographical reference for understanding the Panchayat within Ernakulam district, Kerala.

A Central Kerala Riverine Landscape

Kalady is situated in the central part of Kerala along the banks of the Periyar River, holding immense cultural, historical, and agricultural significance.

Its geographical setting provides a valuable context for examining settlements, transport connectivity, river hydrology, land use, and other spatial dynamics in the Periyar river basin.
